THE REPEAT-SIGN FAMILY -- WHAT THE ORACLE ACTUALLY DOES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A four-rung A/B-on-input ladder (probe-rs-v0..v3.ly: staff-space override on/off
x line-positions override on/off), rendered on the pinned oracle:

    v0  staff refpoints at 28.7622 / 37.7622 / 46.7622
    v1  ditto          v2  ditto          v3  ditto

EXACTLY 9.0 apart in every rung -- which is `staff-staff-spacing''s basic-distance,
and it does NOT move when the staff-space or line-positions overrides come off.
So the oracle's separation is the SPRING SOLUTION, not the skyline packing: the
overrides change each staff's own line span and nothing about where it sits.

The port's corpus deltas put its staves 8.9544 apart (9.0 - 0.0456), which is what
skyline-distance + padding would give.  ⚠ NOT yet confirmed against the port --
the confirming run needs a build and the cycle-2/3 sweep held the build slot.
